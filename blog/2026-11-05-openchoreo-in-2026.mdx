OpenChoreo joined the CNCF Sandbox on January 6, 2026, a year to the week after its first commit. In March, we shipped OpenChoreo v1.0, our first generally available release ready for production use. Looking back eight months later, we're honoured to see how the wider cloud-native and platform engineering community has come together to drive the project to heights we could only have imagined at the beginning.

In this post, we look back on 2026: what we've achieved together as an open-source community, and where we're going from here.

## A massive thank you to our community

The numbers only tell part of the story. A project that started inside WSO2 is now shaped by more than a thousand contributors, building the next generation of internal developer platforms in the open. This, without a doubt, is the growth we're proudest of. After all, [platforms don't win, ecosystems do](/blog/platforms-dont-win-ecosystems-do).

Whether you've reported an issue, sent a PR, opened a discussion, or even asked a question, know that you've helped shape the project into what it is today. We couldn't have done it without you.

To our **contributors**: every fix, feature, question and thoughtful review made the platform better for everyone who comes after you.

To our **maintainers**: thank you for the late nights and careful reviews, and for keeping the bar high while keeping the door open.

To our **adopters**: thank you for trusting our platform in production, for sharing your stories as inspiration to others evaluating the platform, and most of all, for telling us honestly what worked and what didn't. Your feedback shaped our roadmap more than any plan we wrote ourselves.

> If you're trialing OpenChoreo or already running it in production, please consider adding your organization to our [ADOPTERS list](https://github.com/openchoreo/openchoreo/blob/main/ADOPTERS.md). It's a small step that makes a big difference to the project's future within the CNCF ecosystem.

## Where we're heading

Our next minor release, v1.4, is already in the works and we expect to ship it by the end of the year. It focuses on two main themes:

1. **Making AI a first-class actor in the platform**, with AgentTypes, Agents, and recognition of MCP and A2A endpoints, alongside better integrations with agentic runtimes on Kubernetes.

2. **Making brownfield adoption easier**, so that teams with an existing Kubernetes estate can progressively onboard to their new IDP from where they are today.

There's a lot more planned for v1.4, and we encourage you to take a look at our [public roadmap on GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/openchoreo/projects/5/views/2).

We also welcome new ideas and proposals through [GitHub discussions](https://github.com/openchoreo/openchoreo/discussions), [issues](https://github.com/openchoreo/openchoreo/issues) or the [#openchoreo channel on CNCF Slack](https://openchoreo.dev/slack/).
